Boone Bowles

Assistant Professor of Finance

Adam C. Sinn '00 Department of Finance

Mays Business School | Texas A&M University

boone.bowles@tamu.edu | Curriculum Vitae

I research asset pricing and institutional investors, with projects on stock-market anomalies, mutual funds, short selling, information acquisition, and financial social media.

I teach corporate finance in the Full-Time MBA, Anthony Bahr ’91 Engineering MBA, and Flex-Online MBA programs. I also created Boone’s Finance Boot Camp, a free, self-paced finance curriculum.

I earned my Ph.D. in finance at the University of North Carolina and my M.S. in financial economics and B.S. in economics at Utah State University.

I like to exercise, read, and be with my wife and children.
