Boone’s Finance Boot Camp

Boone’s Finance Boot Camp is a public, self-paced finance fundamentals curriculum featuring 19 short instructional videos, companion notes, Excel workbooks, interactive calculator tools, and auto-graded quizzes drawn from large question banks with immediate explanatory feedback. VB—Virtual Boone, an LLM-powered teaching assistant trained on the Boot Camp materials, provides individualized support. The Boot Camp covers time value of money, bonds, financial statements, free cash flow, and NPV.

Boone’s Finance Boot Camp was made possible by the generous support of the Montague family and the Texas A&M Center for Teaching Excellence through the Montague-Center for Teaching Excellence Scholar Award.
