Teaching Innovation and Curriculum Development

Teaching Innovation | Boone’s Finance Boot Camp

Created a public, self-paced finance curriculum with 19 videos, notes, Excel workbooks, calculators, and quizzes with explanatory feedback. Integrated VB—Virtual Boone, a tutor grounded in the Boot Camp materials. Covers time value of money, bonds, financial statements, free cash flow, and NPV. Developed through the Montague–Center for Teaching Excellence Scholar Award.

Curriculum Development | Flex-Online MBA Core Finance

Selected to design and launch the eight-week core finance course, combining financial valuation, live and asynchronous instruction, applied assignments, and interactive tools.
