Teaching Innovations

Boone’s finance boot camp

  • Boone’s finance boot camp is a public, self-paced finance fundamentals curriculum featuring 19 short instructional videos, companion notes, Excel workbooks, interactive calculator tools, and auto-graded quizzes drawn from large question banks with immediate explanatory feedback. VB—Virtual Boone, an LLM-powered teaching assistant trained on the Boot Camp materials, provides students with individualized support. The curriculum covers time value of money, bonds, financial statements, free cash flow, and NPV. The project was developed through the Montague–Center for Teaching Excellence Scholar Award.

Flex-online MBA core finance curriculum development

  • Selected to design and launch the eight-week core finance course for Mays Business School’s Flex-Online MBA program. Built the course around financial valuation, integrating synchronous and asynchronous instruction, applied assignments, and interactive learning tools.
