FINC 612: Corporate Finance

Full-Time MBA | Anthony Bahr ’91 Engineering MBA

This course develops tools for evaluating investment and financing decisions. Students apply financial concepts and valuation methods to assess projects, allocate capital, and understand how firms create value.

This is an abbreviated course description. See the official Texas A&M course catalog for the full description.
