FINC 381: Money & Capital Markets

This course examines money, financial markets, and financial institutions. The first half of the course covers interest rates, the Federal Reserve, monetary policy, and financial crises. The second half explores markets, financial instruments, banking, and other relevant topics. This course demands analytical thinking and the application of economic concepts.
